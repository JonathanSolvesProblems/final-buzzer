Cut-felt appliqué, the way a gym's championship pennants and varsity chenille patches are made.
Each shape is a piece of flat felt cut with scissors, slightly uneven edges, laid on top of the others.
Visible running stitches in thread along the edges of the larger pieces.
A soft fibre texture in the felt, no gloss.
Flat colours only, no shading, no gradients.
Palette: cream felt, warm black felt, mustard yellow felt, deep burgundy felt, one royal blue thread. Nothing else.
Light: flat, even, photographed straight on, like a pennant laid on a table.
Composition: one subject centred, empty plain background, generous space around it, a slight tilt of two or three degrees.
No people.
Never: maple leaves, leaves, flags, trees, 3D render, glossy plastic, isometric, neon, glow, purple, robots, lightbulbs, rockets, globes, handshakes, red LED digits, screens, text, letters, numbers, logos, watermark, photo of a real gym, children, players.
Avoid: drop shadows, gradients, shiny highlights, outlines drawn with a pen.
